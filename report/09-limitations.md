# Limitations of this research

This section lists every limit of this research (**B**). It also lists every institutional gap that limits it (**A→B**). For each one, it says how the limit affects the figures. A limit of this research is never a failing of the CIAA, the courts or the government. Where an institutional gap causes the limit, the gap is reported as a finding in its own stage, and this section points to it.

## Caused by an institutional gap (A→B)

| Limit on this research | Institutional gap behind it (finding) | Effect on the figures |
|---|---|---|
| No conviction rate per person | The court's public record gives one outcome for a whole case (stage 6) | Trial and appeal rates are per case (trial). 34.3% of defendant entries are in partly upheld cases, where who was convicted is unknown from the record |
| Counts of people are short for early trials | The court's case page lists only part of the defendants for many trials registered 2069–2072 (stage 6) | Defendant counts for 2069–2072 are a floor. 25 of 1,015 counted trials leave out at least one defendant |
| Appeals linked to trials by hand | No public record says which trial an appeal comes from (stage 7) | 726 of 4,430 appeals could not be linked, so per-trial appeal rates leave them out. Links rest on judgments, the CIAA's register and names |
| No reason for any closure or any decision not to appeal | The CIAA publishes counts only (stages 1–5, 7) | We can say how many and when, never why. The cause of the 2077/78 turn in closures is open |
| No age for the complaint backlog | The CIAA's reports give the number waiting, not how long (stages 1–3) | The backlog is a count, not a delay |
| No recovery rate for corruption | No ledger of fines and बिगो for corruption judgments (stage 9) | Only the all-case ledger is reported; the 1.6% बिगो figure is from the press |
| Acquittals with no appeal "found", not "proved" | No public list of the CIAA's decisions not to appeal (stage 7) | 219 of 1,010 is "no government appeal found in any record", not proof that none exists |

## Limits of this research alone (B)

| Limit | Effect on the figures |
|---|---|
| **Snapshot dates.** Court records as held on 2 November 2026; Supreme Court case pages read on 1–3 November 2026; appeal links as they stood on 3 November 2026 | Later decisions are not counted. Each figure carries its own date |
| **Open appeals.** About half the appeals registered have no decision yet | The decided-only median wait (3.8 years) is a floor; the 4.1-year figure assumes open appeals behave like decided ones of the same age |
| **Hearing pages.** Case pages were read for appeals numbered 070-CR- onward (99.6% coverage) and only 11.7% of older ones | Hearing figures are given for the 070-CR- appeals; older years rest on a selected sample |
| **Court outcomes missing in our copy.** 238 appeals that the court page shows as decided have no decision date in our copy of the court record. 160 take their outcome from the court page | A few decided appeals are counted as open |
| **Judgments without a text.** 126 of 1,550 acquittal trials, and some appeal judgments, have no text we could read | Grounds for acquittal and reversal come from the trials with a text |
| **Readings made with AI software, not yet checked by a lawyer.** Why convictions were reversed (16 judgments), why the court acquits (31 of a sample of 42) and the 12 selective-prosecution judgments | Stated as "in the judgments we read". The quoted passages are kept so that a lawyer can check each reading. These readings of court judgments were made with AI software and have not yet been checked by a lawyer. They may contain errors; the judgment itself is the authority. |
| **Small samples.** Reversal grounds: 16. Acquittal grounds: 31 | Shares are not rates for all cases |
| **Keyword searches.** Selective prosecution was found by searching for its wording | The 12 judgments are a minimum |
| **Defendant entries are accusations.** About 5% carry an identity code | Someone charged twice counts twice |
| **Illicit enrichment.** The per-case link between the CIAA's and the courts' arithmetic cannot be counted from our data | Stage 5 reports the difference in conviction rates only |
| **CIAA series breaks.** FY2072/73 is missing from its own report, and earlier years use different tables | Trends start where the series is consistent |
| **Press figure.** The 1.6% बिगो recovery is from the press, attributed to the Judgment Execution Directorate | Reported as a press figure only |
